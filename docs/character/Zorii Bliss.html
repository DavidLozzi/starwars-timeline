Zorii Bliss

Ultimate Star Wars Timeline › Characters › Zorii Bliss

Hidden behind a bronzium-finished helmet and a voice-masking vocoder, Zorii Bliss was the feared leader of the Spice Runners of Kijimi and one of the few people who knew Poe Dameron's past as a criminal. She grew up in the smuggling outfit her mother Zeva ran and fell for Poe when he joined as a teenager. Their relationship fell apart at the Kijimi summit, when he turned down her offer to take the gang over together and walked away. Zorii went on to take control of the Spice Runners herself. Years later, with the First Order occupying Kijimi, she was saving up to escape on a Y-wing she named the Comeuppance. When Poe came back with Rey, Finn, Chewbacca and C-3PO in search of the way to Exegol, she met him at blaster point. Rey's fighting skill won her over, and she took the group to the droidsmith Babu Frik and gave Poe a captain's medallion so he could board a Star Destroyer. She turned down his offer to join the Resistance, but she escaped Kijimi before a Xyston-class Star Destroyer destroyed the planet. She then flew the Comeuppance with Babu Frik at the Battle of Exegol and celebrated the victory on Ajan Kloss, sharing a knowing look with Poe across the crowd.

~0 BBY - Birth on Kijimi

Zorii is born into the Bliss family, sometime between 1 BBY and 1 ABY. Her mother Zeva leads the Spice Runners of Kijimi, taking over from Zarah Bliss.

~5 ABY - Joins the Spice Runners

While still young, Zorii joins her mother's spice-running crew on Kijimi.

~18 ABY - Meets Poe Dameron

Teenage pilot Poe Dameron joins the Spice Runners. He and Zorii start a romance.

~18 ABY - The Kijimi Summit

Zeva plans to wipe out rival families at a fake summit. Zorii, who also opposes her mother, asks Poe to take over the gang with her. He says no and leaves Kijimi, and her feelings for him turn to fury. She tells him never to come back.

Before 34 ABY - Leader of the Spice Runners

Zorii takes command of the Spice Runners and becomes feared by her enemies and respected by her crew.

~34 ABY - First Order Occupation

After the Battle of Crait, the First Order takes over Kijimi. Tired of the suffering she sees everywhere, Zorii buys a BTA-NR2 Y-wing for about 40,000 credits and names it the Comeuppance, planning to leave.

35 ABY - Reunion with Poe

Poe comes back to Kijimi with Resistance agents looking for Exegol. Zorii and her goons confront him, but Rey's skill impresses her and she agrees to help.

35 ABY - Babu Frik's Workshop

Zorii takes the group to the droidsmith Babu Frik, who unlocks the forbidden Sith translation in C-3PO's memory. On the rooftop she talks with Poe about the occupation, encourages him to keep fighting and briefly lifts part of her helmet.

35 ABY - The Captain's Medallion

Zorii warns the group that First Order troops are coming and gives Poe a captain's medallion, at great cost to herself, so he can get aboard the Steadfast. She turns down his offer to come with them and stays on Kijimi.

35 ABY - Escape from Kijimi

Darth Sidious orders Kijimi destroyed by a Xyston-class Star Destroyer's axial superlaser. Zorii gets away before the planet is obliterated.

35 ABY - Battle of Exegol

Zorii flies the Comeuppance with Babu Frik alongside the Resistance and the Citizens' Fleet against the Sith Eternal's Final Order fleet.

35 ABY - Victory on Ajan Kloss

Zorii joins the Resistance's victory celebration and shares a look with Poe across the crowd.
