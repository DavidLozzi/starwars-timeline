Mando

Ultimate Star Wars Timeline › Characters › Mando

Known across the Outer Rim simply as "the Mandalorian," Din Djarin was a feared bounty hunter who threw away his livelihood to save the child he had been hired to capture. Orphaned as a boy when Separatist droids destroyed his village during the Clone Wars, he was carried off by a Death Watch warrior and raised as a foundling on Concordia. He took the creed of the Children of the Watch and swore never to remove his helmet in front of another living being. In the years after the Empire fell he became one of the Bounty Hunters' Guild's best hunters, working out of Nevarro in his gunship, the Razor Crest. In 9 ABY an Imperial remnant hired him to retrieve a mysterious asset. It turned out to be Grogu, a Force-sensitive infant of Yoda's species, and Djarin rescued him from his Imperial clients instead of handing him over. That turned the Guild and Moff Gideon against him. With allies like Greef Karga, Cara Dune, Kuiil and IG-11 he fought his way free. The Armorer then named him and Grogu a clan of two and charged him with finding the child's own kind. That search brought him to Bo-Katan Kryze, Ahsoka Tano and Boba Fett. It ended with Djarin beating Gideon, taking the Darksaber, and taking off his helmet to say goodbye as Luke Skywalker took Grogu away. The Armorer cast him out as an apostate. He fought beside Boba Fett at Mos Espa and got Grogu back. He then bathed in the Living Waters beneath Mandalore to redeem himself, and helped Bo-Katan unite the Mandalorians and retake their homeworld, where Gideon was killed. After that he formally adopted Grogu as his son and apprentice and settled on Nevarro, taking contracts from the New Republic to hunt down Imperial warlords. Those jobs later pitted him against the warlord Barro, the Hutt Twins and the Imperial commander Janu Coin.

~30 BBY - Birth on Aq Vetina

Din Djarin is born on Aq Vetina, late in the life of the Galactic Republic. Canon only says he was born before 19 BBY.

~20 BBY - Orphaned in the Clone Wars

Separatist battle droids attack his village. His parents hide him in a hatch and are then killed. A super battle droid finds him, but a Death Watch warrior destroys it and flies the boy to safety.

~20 BBY - Raised as a Foundling

The Mandalorians take him in as a foundling. He grows up on Concordia, trains in the Fighting Corps and becomes part of Death Watch.

Before 9 ABY - Joining the Tribe

After the Great Purge of Mandalore he joins the Tribe, a Children of the Watch covert hidden on Nevarro. He receives his armor and swears never to show his face to anyone.

Before 9 ABY - Mercenary Work with Ranzar Malk

He works alongside Ranzar Malk and the Twi'lek siblings Xi'an and Qin, and has a relationship with Xi'an. The crew falls apart after Qin is captured.

Before 9 ABY - Guild Bounty Hunter

Flying the Razor Crest, he becomes one of the Bounty Hunters' Guild's top hunters, taking pucks from Greef Karga on Nevarro.

9 ABY - The Mythrol Bounty

He tracks down a Mythrol fugitive on the ice world Pagodon, freezes him in carbonite and collects the payment on Nevarro.

9 ABY - The Client's Commission

An Imperial remnant officer known as the Client hires him to bring in an unnamed asset, paying a beskar ingot up front. The Armorer forges it into a pauldron.

9 ABY - Finding the Child on Arvala-7

With help from the Ugnaught Kuiil and the droid IG-11, he storms a Nikto camp and finds the asset: an infant of Yoda's species. He shoots IG-11 to keep it from killing the child.

9 ABY - The Mudhorn

To buy back his ship's parts from the Jawas, he fights a mudhorn for its egg. The child saves him by lifting the beast with the Force.

9 ABY - Rescuing the Child

He hands the child over for a camtono of beskar and a full armor set, then turns around, raids the Imperial compound and takes the child back. The Tribe comes out of hiding to cover his escape from the Guild.

9 ABY - Sorgan

While lying low on Sorgan, he and Cara Dune train villagers who then destroy a raider gang's AT-ST.

9 ABY - The Prison Ship Job

He joins Ranzar Malk's crew to free Qin from a New Republic prison ship. When the crew betrays him, he leaves them locked in cells.

9 ABY - Battle of Nevarro

Karga lays a trap, and he fights Moff Gideon's forces alongside Karga, Dune, Kuiil and a reprogrammed IG-11. Kuiil and IG-11 are killed. The Armorer names him and the child a clan of two, Clan Mudhorn, and tells him to find the child's own kind.

9 ABY - Tatooine and the Krayt Dragon

He meets Marshal Cobb Vanth in Mos Pelgo, and together with the Tusken Raiders they kill a krayt dragon. Vanth gives him Boba Fett's armor in return.

9 ABY - Meeting Bo-Katan Kryze

While escorting the Frog Lady to Trask, he meets Bo-Katan Kryze and learns the Children of the Watch are a fringe sect. He helps her raid an Imperial freighter, and she tells him to look for Ahsoka Tano on Corvus.

9 ABY - Ahsoka Tano on Corvus

He helps Ahsoka free Calodan from Magistrate Morgan Elsbeth. Ahsoka learns the child's name is Grogu, but she refuses to train him.

9 ABY - Grogu Taken on Tython

At the seeing stone on Tython he meets Boba Fett and Fennec Shand. Dark troopers kidnap Grogu, and Gideon's forces destroy the Razor Crest.

9 ABY - Morak

With Migs Mayfeld, he infiltrates an Imperial refinery to get Gideon's coordinates, taking off his helmet to pass a facial scan.

9 ABY - Rescue of Grogu

He boards Gideon's light cruiser with Bo-Katan, Koska Reeves, Dune and Fennec. He defeats Gideon and wins the Darksaber. Luke Skywalker arrives, and Djarin takes off his helmet to say goodbye before Grogu leaves with him.

9 ABY - Declared an Apostate

Back with the surviving covert, he admits he removed his helmet, and the Armorer casts him out. He replaces the Razor Crest with a modified N-1 starfighter.

9 ABY - Battle of Mos Espa

He fights for Boba Fett against the Pyke Syndicate on Tatooine. Grogu, choosing him over Jedi training, joins him there.

9 ABY - Bathing in the Living Waters

He goes into the mines of Mandalore and bathes in the Living Waters, which restores his standing with the Armorer. Bo-Katan saves him, and she gains the Darksaber when she defeats the creature that had disarmed him.

9 ABY - Plazir-15 and the Pirate Attack

He fights alongside the Armorer's covert to repel Gorian Shard's pirates from Nevarro and serves the Duchess of Plazir-15.

9 ABY - Liberation of Mandalore

With Bo-Katan and the reunited Mandalorians, he retakes Mandalore from the Imperial Remnant, and Moff Gideon is killed.

9 ABY - Din Grogu

He formally adopts Grogu as his son and apprentice, and they settle in a cabin on Nevarro. He takes work as an independent contractor for the New Republic.

After 9 ABY - Hunting Barro

Years later, working with Garazeb Orrelios, he hunts the Imperial warlord Barro on Phostera and kills him. The New Republic rewards him with a new Razor Crest.

After 9 ABY - Mission to Shakari

Colonel Ward sends him after Rotta the Hutt, who has become a gladiator on Shakari. There he discovers that crime lord Janu is really Imperial Commander Coin and captures him with Zeb's help.

After 9 ABY - Battle of Nal Hutta

Embo captures him and hands him to the Hutt Twins. Grogu and Minch free him, and he leads the assault on the Hutt palace to rescue Rotta while New Republic forces attack from orbit. Afterward he and Grogu fly off again in the new Razor Crest.
