Plo Koon

Ultimate Star Wars Timeline › Characters › Plo Koon

Plo Koon died in 19 BBY.

Plo Koon was a Jedi Council master and one of the Republic's best starfighter pilots during the Clone Wars. He was known above all for how much he cared about the clone troopers under his command. As a young master he found a three-year-old Ahsoka Tano on Shili and brought her to the Jedi Temple, and he stayed a protective mentor to her for the rest of his life. He trained Bultar Swan to knighthood and sat on the Council that met Anakin Skywalker. He fought in the arena at Geonosis and survived, then took command of the 104th "Wolfpack" Battalion alongside Commander Wolffe. After his fleet was wiped out by the Malevolence's ion cannon, he flew escort for the attack that crippled the warship. He also escorted the evacuation of Felucia, hunted down a young Boba Fett, led the fleet that pulled the strike team out of the Citadel, and helped retake Felucia. He was part of the Council that expelled Ahsoka after the Temple bombing and later invited her to return. In the last days of the war he won the Battle of Cato Neimoidia. When Order 66 was issued, his own ARC-170 escort shot his fighter down over the planet's bridge cities, and he died in the crash.

Unknown - Birth on Dorin

Born on the Kel Dor homeworld. Canon gives no year. He was later taken into the Jedi Order and became the Padawan of Master Tyvokka.

34 BBY - Rescue on Oosalon

Arrives on Oosalon with Depa Billaba and Qui-Gon Jinn to back up Mace Windu after Windu defeats Warlord Guattako's army of kidnapped children.

33 BBY - Discovers Ahsoka Tano

On Shili he finds the three-year-old Togruta Ahsoka Tano, who had just escaped a false Jedi, and brings her to the Jedi Temple. The two stay close for the rest of his life.

~33 BBY - Council mission to Kwenn

Now a Council master, he travels to Kwenn with Saesee Tiin. They settle a labor dispute at Kwikhaul, help rescue a beached oroko, and he coordinates the taxi fleet that evacuates civilians during the pirate attack on the Bicentennial Celebration.

32 BBY - Naboo Crisis

Sits on the Council that doubts the Sith have returned and refuses to let Qui-Gon Jinn train Anakin Skywalker. After Jinn's death he attends the funeral on Naboo.

~22 BBY - Bultar Swan knighted

His Padawan Bultar Swan becomes a Jedi Knight shortly before the war.

22 BBY - First Battle of Geonosis

Joins the 212-member Jedi assault team in the Petranaki Arena, is among the survivors rescued by Yoda and the clone army, and fights in the first battle of the Clone Wars.

22 BBY - Hunting the Malevolence

Leads the 104th Battalion from the Triumphant. The Malevolence's ion cannon disables his fleet at Abregado. He holds out in an escape pod with Wolffe, Sinker and Boost until Anakin and Ahsoka rescue them.

22 BBY - Battle of the Kaliida Nebula

Flies the Blade of Dorin as escort for Shadow Squadron through a neebray herd. The squadron wrecks the Malevolence's ion cannon, and he then helps command the pursuit that ends with the ship's destruction.

21 BBY - Evacuation of Felucia

Leads fighters that break through the Separatist blockade to cover the gunships evacuating Kenobi, Skywalker and Tano's troops.

~21 BBY - Hunt for Boba Fett

Rescues Mace Windu and Anakin from the wreck of the Endurance on Vanqor. With Ahsoka he tracks Aurra Sing to Florrum, captures Boba Fett, and gets the hostages freed.

~21 BBY - Rescue from the Citadel

Briefs the strike team sent to free Even Piell, then leads the fleet to Lola Sayu that extracts the survivors. Afterward he covers for Ahsoka's unauthorized part in the mission.

~20 BBY - Second Battle of Felucia

Leads an assault on a droid outpost with Anakin and Ahsoka. When Trandoshan hunters kidnap Ahsoka, he alerts Republic forces to search for her and later welcomes her home.

~20 BBY - Rescue of Adi Gallia

Leads the Wolfpack aboard General Grievous's ship to free Adi Gallia.

19 BBY - Ahsoka's trial and departure

Sits on the Council that expels Ahsoka after the Temple hangar bombing. Once Barriss Offee is exposed, he and the Council offer to take her back, and she declines.

19 BBY - Battle of Cato Neimoidia

Commands the 442nd Siege Battalion to victory in the last days of the war.

19 BBY - Death during Order 66

While he flies over the bridge cities of Cato Neimoidia, his own ARC-170 wingmen open fire when Order 66 is issued. His starfighter is shot down and he dies in the crash.
