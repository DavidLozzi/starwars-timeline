Temmin Wexley was born in 11 BBY and died in 35 ABY.

Temmin "Snap" Wexley went from teenage junk dealer to one of the most dependable wingmen in the Resistance, and he fought in the battles that ended both the Empire and the First Order. His father was taken by the Empire and his mother Norra left to fly for the Rebellion, so Temmin grew up on his own in Myrra. He ran a scrap shop out of the family home, did business with local gangsters, and rebuilt a battle droid, Mister Bones, into his fiercely loyal bodyguard. When Norra came home after Endor, the two reconciled during the uprising that freed Akiva from Imperial control. Temmin then joined her team as they hunted Imperial war criminals, learned to fly under Wedge Antilles (who later became his stepfather), and helped free Chewbacca and other prisoners from Ashmead's Lock, where he found his father again. The reunion ended badly when Brentin, controlled by an Imperial implant, took part in the Liberation Day attack on Chandrila. Temmin fought at the Battle of Jakku and went on to serve as a New Republic pilot. Decades later he joined the Resistance, flew with Black Squadron, and became a captain and recon specialist in Blue Squadron at Takodana and Starkiller Base. He married fellow pilot Karé Kun and helped rebuild the Resistance after Crait. He was killed in the Battle of Exegol, and Poe Dameron remembered him as the best wingman he ever had.

11 BBY - Birth on Akiva

Born to Norra and Brentin Lore Wexley and raised in the city of Myrra.

~5 BBY - Father arrested

Imperial forces arrest Brentin for carrying messages for the Rebel Alliance, and Temmin loses his father while still a young child.

~1 ABY - Mother joins the Rebellion

At about twelve, Temmin is left with his aunt Esmelle and her wife Shirene when Norra leaves Akiva to become a Rebel pilot. He soon moves back into the family home to live on his own.

~2 ABY - Junk dealer and Mister Bones

Turns the family house into a spare-parts shop, gets involved with the gangster Surat Nuat, and rebuilds a B1 battle droid into his bodyguard, Mister Bones.

3 ABY - Meets Kay Vess

After Surat's gang robs him, Temmin makes a deal with the outlaw Kay Vess: he fits a hydro-repulsor to her speeder in exchange for the durasteel struts she recovers from the gang.

4 ABY - Reunion with Norra

After Endor, Norra comes back to Akiva. Temmin is still bitter that she left, but he is pulled into the fight when Surat's thugs capture him and Jas Emari and Sinjir Rath Velus rescue him.

4 ABY - Rebellion on Akiva

Helps seize an Imperial communications station and broadcast a call to revolt. He secretly tries to trade his companions to Surat for safe passage, is betrayed and thrown from the Satrap's palace, then redeems himself by freeing his mother and friends aboard the Golden Harp. Akiva is liberated and joins the New Republic.

4 ABY - Joins the hunt for Imperials

Reconciles with Norra on Chandrila, joins her team to track down Imperial war criminals, and begins flight training under Wedge Antilles.

5 ABY - Capture of Perwin Gedde

Flies the getaway ship Halo on Vorlag and shoots down two pursuing TIE fighters. Wedge offers him a future place in Phantom Squadron.

5 ABY - Rescue at Ashmead's Lock

Joins Han Solo to storm Golas Aram's compound on Irudiru, then helps free Chewbacca and the other prisoners from the automated prison on Kashyyyk. Among the freed prisoners is his father Brentin.

5 ABY - Attack on Chandrila

Uncovers the Imperial agent Windom Traducier but is stunned and hidden by his implant-controlled father. On Liberation Day he confronts Brentin during the assassination attempt on Mon Mothma and stops him from turning the gun on himself.

5 ABY - Hunt for Rae Sloane

Goes with Norra's team across the galaxy looking for Grand Admiral Sloane. After the capture of Mercurial Swift, the trail leads to Jakku.

5 ABY - Battle of Jakku

Fights in the battle that ends the Galactic Empire.

~5 ABY - New Republic pilot

Joins the New Republic Defense Fleet as a starfighter pilot and later serves in the New Republic's precision air team.

~34 ABY - Joins the Resistance

Is recruited into the Resistance and flies in Poe Dameron's Black Squadron alongside Karé Kun.

34 ABY - Battle of Takodana

As a captain in Blue Squadron, flies in the Resistance strike that relieves Takodana.

34 ABY - Destruction of Starkiller Base

Briefs the Resistance on Starkiller Base's defenses and flies in the attack that destroys it.

34 ABY - Rebuilding the Resistance

Is away on a mission during the evacuation of D'Qar and Crait. Afterwards he helps rebuild the Resistance, including recruiting on Corellia.

35 ABY - Kijimi

Takes part in the Resistance operation that rescues the heroes' allies on Kijimi.

35 ABY - Death at the Battle of Exegol

Is killed while flying in the Resistance fleet against the Sith Eternal at Exegol. Poe Dameron later delivers his eulogy.
