Sabine Wren

Ultimate Star Wars Timeline › Characters › Sabine Wren

Sabine Wren was born in 21 BBY.

Sabine Wren is the graffiti-slinging demolitions expert of the Ghost crew, whose painted starbird became the emblem of the Rebel Alliance. Raised in Clan Wren during the Empire's grip on Mandalore, she built the Arc Pulse Generator as an Imperial cadet. When she saw the weapon turned against her own people, she deserted, and the break left her estranged from her family. After a stint as a bounty hunter with Ketsu Onyo, she joined Hera Syndulla and Kanan Jarrus's rebel cell and harried the Empire across Lothal and beyond. In 2 BBY she found the Darksaber on Dathomir and learned to wield it. She used it to reconcile with her mother, Ursa, helped bring down Viceroy Gar Saxon, and rallied the clans against the Empire before giving the blade to Bo-Katan Kryze and destroying her own weapon for good. She fought in the liberation of Lothal, where she lost Kanan and saw Ezra Bridger vanish with Thrawn, then stayed behind to guard the planet. She later trained under Ahsoka Tano, but they drifted apart after the Empire's purge of Mandalore killed her family. In 9 ABY she crossed into another galaxy, found Ezra on Peridea, and was left stranded there as Thrawn escaped. On Peridea she began to reach the Force.

21 BBY - Birth on Mandalore

Born into Clan Wren of House Vizsla near the end of the Clone Wars, while Mandalore is in turmoil. Her mother, Ursa, belongs to Death Watch. The family's ancestral home is Krownest.

~7 BBY - Imperial Academy of Mandalore

Enrolls as a cadet at the Imperial Academy on occupied Mandalore and designs the Arc Pulse Generator, nicknamed "the Duchess," which turns beskar armor against the person wearing it.

~6 BBY - Desertion from the Empire

Horrified to see her weapon used against Mandalorians, she speaks out, destroys the prototype and flees with Ketsu Onyo. The Empire and her own clan brand her a traitor.

~6 BBY - Bounty hunter

Works as a bounty hunter with Ketsu Onyo until Ketsu leaves her for dead and the partnership ends badly.

~5 BBY - Joins the Spectres

At about 16, she is recruited into Hera Syndulla and Kanan Jarrus's rebel cell aboard the Ghost and takes the call sign Spectre 5.

5 BBY - The Artist of Lothal

Paints the starbird on a TIE fighter at a Capital City airfield and escapes behind a paint bomb. She helps free enslaved Wookiees from Kessel and welcomes Ezra Bridger to the crew.

5 BBY - Missions across Lothal

Takes part in the Stygeon Prime rescue attempt, patches up her rift with Hera at Fort Anaxes, frees slaves on Oon, and joins the Empire Day sabotage and the operation to get Tseebo off Lothal.

5 BBY - Siege of Lothal and rebel fleet

Helps rescue Kanan from Mustafar. After the Siege of Lothal, the Spectres join Phoenix Squadron and the wider rebel movement.

~4 BBY - Mandalorian encounters

Reunites with Ketsu Onyo and helps her break from the Empire. She also clashes with the Mandalorian Protectors of Concord Dawn under Fenn Rau.

3 BBY - Imperial Super Commandos

Fights Gar Saxon's Imperial Super Commandos on Concord Dawn and learns her brother Tristan serves among them.

2 BBY - The Darksaber

Finds the Darksaber in Maul's lair on Dathomir. Kanan and Fenn Rau train her to use it.

2 BBY - Return to Krownest

Goes home to Krownest and wins over her family. She defeats Gar Saxon in a duel but spares him, and her mother kills him when he tries to shoot Sabine in the back.

2 BBY - Battle of Atollon

Leads Mandalorian reinforcements to help the rebels at Chopper Base during the Imperial assault.

1 BBY - Mandalorian Civil War

Rescues her father, frees Mandalore from Imperial control, destroys the Duchess weapon and all her research, and gives the Darksaber to Bo-Katan Kryze.

1 BBY - Liberation of Lothal

Returns to Lothal with the Ghost crew. She fights through Kanan's death and Grand Admiral Thrawn's occupation, and the planet is freed after Ezra disappears into hyperspace with the Chimaera.

After 1 BBY - Guardian of Lothal

Stays on Lothal to protect it, living in Ezra's old comm tower.

~4 ABY - Apprentice to Ahsoka Tano

After the Empire falls, she sets out with Ahsoka Tano to search for Ezra and trains as her apprentice. The two later part ways after Mandalore's purge kills Sabine's family.

9 ABY - Search for Ezra Bridger

Ahsoka recruits her again to decode a star map. Sabine is badly wounded by Shin Hati and then gives the map to Baylan Skoll in exchange for a chance to find Ezra.

9 ABY - Peridea

Travels to another galaxy with Morgan Elsbeth's forces, reunites with Ezra, and is left stranded on Peridea with Ahsoka when Thrawn escapes. There she begins to use the Force.
