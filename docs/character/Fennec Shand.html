Fennec Shand

Ultimate Star Wars Timeline › Characters › Fennec Shand

Fennec Shand is an elite mercenary and Master Assassin who went from a young newcomer in the bounty-hunting trade to Boba Fett's most trusted partner. She broke into the business just after the Clone Wars. One of her first big jobs was a secret commission from the Kaminoan scientist Nala Se to recover the clone girl Omega. That hunt pitted her against Clone Force 99 on Pantora and against the veteran hunter Cad Bane at Bora Vio, where she outfought him. Over the Imperial years she built a reputation as one of the most feared and expensive guns for hire in the underworld, working for syndicates including the Hutts. After the Empire fell, the New Republic jailed many of her employers and she went on the run with a price on her head. In 9 ABY, Din Djarin and the rookie Toro Calican caught her in the Tatooine desert, and Calican shot her and left her to die. Boba Fett found her, paid to have her rebuilt with cybernetic organs, and won her loyalty. She helped him reclaim Slave I, recover his armor on Tython, and rescue Grogu from Moff Gideon's cruiser. She then stood beside him as he took Jabba's old palace and became daimyo of Mos Espa, acting as his enforcer, strategist and right hand through the war against the Pyke Syndicate.

19 BBY - Enters the Bounty Hunting Trade

With the Clone Wars just over, Shand begins working as a bounty hunter and gun for hire. She quickly earns a reputation for cunning and ruthlessness, and even Cad Bane takes notice.

19 BBY - Hired to Capture Omega

The Kaminoan scientist Nala Se secretly commissions Shand to bring in the clone girl Omega, who has fled Kamino with Clone Force 99, so that Omega stays out of Lama Su's hands.

19 BBY - Chase on Pantora

Acting on a tip from Raspar Six, Shand tracks Omega to Ro Station, wins the girl's trust and then fights Hunter and Wrecker. A skyway chase ends with her stolen airspeeder destroyed and Omega getting away.

19 BBY - Showdown with Cad Bane at Bora Vio

Shand kills Taun We to stop Bane's handoff of Omega. She fights Bane through the abandoned cloning facility, knocks him over a balcony and sabotages his ship, while the Bad Batch rescues Omega. Nala Se pays her for the job.

~18 BBY - Hunt for Sylar Saris

In exchange for information, Hunter and Wrecker help Shand pursue the fugitive bounty hunter Sylar Saris through the swamps of Veelo.

Imperial Era - Master Assassin

Shand becomes one of the galaxy's most feared and expensive mercenaries, working for the top crime syndicates, including the Hutt Clan, and becomes wanted by the Imperial Security Bureau.

3 ABY - Too Expensive to Hire

After the Battle of Hoth, the bondsman Syphacc hires the pit fighter Tasu Leech instead of Shand to hunt Beilert Valance, because she costs more.

~5 ABY - On the Run

After the Empire's defeat at Jakku, the New Republic imprisons many of her employers. Shand becomes a fugitive with a 10,000-credit bounty on her head.

9 ABY - Captured Beyond the Dune Sea

Hiding on Tatooine, Shand kills a pursuing hunter, then holds off Din Djarin and Toro Calican until a night assault forces her to surrender.

9 ABY - Shot by Toro Calican

Shand talks Calican into turning on Djarin, but the rookie shoots her in the stomach and leaves her for dead.

9 ABY - Saved by Boba Fett

Boba Fett finds her dying, has a Mos Eisley mod artist replace her ruined organs with cybernetics, and brings her back to health. Shand pledges herself to his service.

9 ABY - Reclaiming Slave I

Shand helps Fett infiltrate Jabba's palace to take back Slave I from Bib Fortuna. They then search the Sarlacc pit for his armor, and she kills the Sarlacc with a seismic charge.

9 ABY - Confrontation on Tython

With Fett, Shand confronts Din Djarin at the Force-henge to reclaim Fett's armor. The three fight off Moff Gideon's stormtroopers, but Dark Troopers carry Grogu away.

9 ABY - Raid on Morak

Shand provides sniper cover alongside Cara Dune while Djarin and Migs Mayfeld infiltrate the Imperial refinery to find Gideon's cruiser.

9 ABY - Rescue of Grogu

Shand joins Bo-Katan Kryze, Koska Reeves and Cara Dune in storming Gideon's light cruiser and taking the bridge. Luke Skywalker arrives and leaves with Grogu.

9 ABY - Taking Jabba's Palace

Back on Tatooine, Shand stands beside Fett as he kills Bib Fortuna and claims the throne. She becomes his chief lieutenant as he takes over as daimyo of Mos Espa.

9 ABY - Defending the Daimyo

Shand fights off the Night Wind assassins who ambush Fett in Mos Espa. She advises him through his dealings with the Hutt twins, Black Krrsantan and Mayor Mok Shaiz.

9 ABY - Recruiting the Mods

Shand brings a gang of cybernetically modified youths into Fett's service as enforcers.

9 ABY - War with the Pyke Syndicate

During the fight for Mos Espa, Shand kills the mayor and the crime bosses who betrayed Fett, then fights alongside Fett, Din Djarin and the Mods until the Pykes are defeated.
