Logan Howlett, born 1832

Logan is a nearly immortal mutant with rapid healing and adamantium-laced claws, forged by decades of black-ops experimentation and personal loss into the X-Men's most reluctant hero. He fights alongside Xavier's students across four decades before a slow-acting poison finally catches up to him in 2029, where he sacrifices himself protecting a new generation of mutants. His long memory ties together Fox's fractured X-Men timeline more than any other character.

Raven Darkhölme, born 1962

Raven Darkhölme is a shapeshifting mutant raised alongside Charles Xavier who becomes a reluctant symbol for mutant liberation after a very public act of heroism in 1973. Torn between Xavier's ideals and Erik's militancy, she spends the next two decades as one of the mutant community's most visible and most hunted figures. She is killed in 1992 during a rescue mission gone wrong, a death that sends the X-Men down a darker path.

Erik Lehnsherr, born 1932

Erik Lehnsherr is a Holocaust survivor turned master of magnetism, his childhood trauma convincing him that mutants must dominate rather than coexist with humanity. He alternates between ally and adversary of Charles Xavier across decades of mutant history, founding and leading the Brotherhood of Mutants through the school's most dangerous years. His fate after the mid-2000s is left open in the films that carry his old friend's story forward to its end.

Charles Xavier, born 1962

Charles Xavier is the telepathic founder of the school for gifted mutants, spending decades trying to build peaceful coexistence between mutants and humanity. His friendship-turned-rivalry with Erik Lehnsherr shapes nearly every major X-Men conflict from the 1960s onward, and the same time-travel event that rewrites their future keeps him at the center of it into old age. He dies in 2029, his final seizure setting off the chain of events that defines Logan's last stand.
